# Credits

## Data
- **Tide predictions and station information:** [NOAA CO-OPS](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/) (Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services).
- **Waves, water temperature and place search:** [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (marine and geocoding APIs).

## Built with
- [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/) and Dart for the app, with Material 3
- Kotlin and Android's widget system for the home screen widgets
- Packages: `fl_chart` (the tide chart), `geolocator` (your location), `http`, `intl` (dates and times), `path_provider`, `shared_preferences`, and for the icon and splash `flutter_launcher_icons` and `flutter_native_splash`
- Android WorkManager for the widgets' timed redraw
